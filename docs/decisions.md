# Decisions and open questions

Format: number, date, question, answer, who decided. Claude adds open questions, the human answers.
Open questions block any task that depends on them.

Questions 1 to 37 were raised by `/write-spec` on 2026-10-22. Story references point to `docs/spec/stories.md`.

## General rule (applies to every question)

**No design, no feature.** Anything not designed in Figma is built visually as designed, with no behavior and no backend logic. Links between existing screens work. Exceptions only for what the app cannot work without (states from #6, auth from #2 and #3). Decided by Marko, 2026-10-22.
