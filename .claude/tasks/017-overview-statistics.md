## Goal
The Overview Statistics card shows the "Weekly Comparison" control, the legend and the bar chart of the signed-in user's expenses per day, this week against last week, Monday to Sunday in their time zone, with no teal bar after today and a $0-based axis whose ticks the backend computes. The data comes from a new read-only endpoint in the shared contract that sums, groups and scales everything in the backend, through `VisibleTransactions` only. Also, from the task 016 gate: every `/api` response carries `Cache-Control: no-store`, and a keyboard Retry in any widget leaves focus in that widget instead of on the page body.

## Scope

- **Shared** (`@finebank/shared`):
  - `shared/src/contract/statistics.ts` (new, exported from the contract index):
    - `weeklyComparisonDaySchema`: `{ date, lastWeek, thisWeek }`: `date` this week's plain date of that weekday (`z.iso.date()`, as bills); `lastWeek` `moneySchema` with `cents` 0 or more (the same weekday a week earlier); `thisWeek` the same, or `null` for a day after today (#55).
    - `weeklyComparisonResponseSchema`: `{ days, ticks }`: `days` exactly 7, Monday first, consecutive dates; `thisWeek` `null` only after the days that have a value (today always has one); `ticks` `null` exactly when every value of both weeks is 0 (the empty state, #55), otherwise 5 amounts from $0 in equal steps whose top is at or above every value (the shape `axisTicks` gives, #91). Unknown keys are dropped.
    - `statisticsContract.weeklyComparison`: `GET /statistics/weekly-comparison`, no body. Task 024 adds the Expenses page's chart to this file.
  - `shared/src/format/date.ts`: `formatDayLabel(date)`: a plain date as the Statistics x label, the two-digit day and the 3-letter en-US weekday: `2026-10-19` gives `19 Mon`, `2026-11-01` gives `01 Sun` (#84, the Figma sample "17 Sun"). It throws a `RangeError` for anything that is not a valid plain date. Display only: no zone (a plain date has none) and no arithmetic on the result.
