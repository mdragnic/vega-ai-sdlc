## Goal
Transactions (107) shows the signed-in user's transactions in Figma's table under the tabs All, Revenue and Expenses: the selected tab's first 10, of any status, dated up to today in their time zone, newest first, each with its category icon and item, the shop, the date, the Payment Method (its account's type) and the signed amount; "Load More" adds the next 10. The rows come from a new read-only, cursor-paged endpoint in the shared contract that applies every tab's filter in the backend, through `VisibleTransactions` only, and whose first 5 rows on each tab are Overview's Recent Transaction rows.

  - `shared/src/contract/transaction.ts` (extended; the direction and status schemas and `transactionsContract.recent` stay):
    - `TRANSACTIONS_PAGE_SIZE` (10, #27, #74).
    - `transactionsTabSchema`: `all`, `revenue`, `expenses` (the Recent Transaction response's keys and tab values).

    - `transactionRowSchema`: `{ item, shopName, category, direction, occurredAt, paymentMethod, amount }`: `recentTransactionSchema`'s keys and rules (the UTC timestamp, cents 0 or more with `USD`, an expense with its category and income with none), written once and shared by both schemas (#74), plus `paymentMethod` (`accountTypeSchema`, #57). Unknown keys dropped. `recentTransactionSchema` parses as before.
    - `transactionsPageSchema`: `{ transactions, nextCursor }`: at most `TRANSACTIONS_PAGE_SIZE` rows, newest first; `nextCursor` the cursor of the next page (1 to 64 characters), or `null` on the last page.
    - `transactionsContract.list`: `GET /transactions`, query `transactionsQuerySchema`, no body, response `transactionsPageSchema`.
- **Backend** (no table, column, migration or provider change):
  - `AccountsService.accountTypes(userId)`: each of the user's accounts' type by its ID, from `BANK_DATA_PROVIDER.listAccounts` (#57, #77). `AccountsModule` exports `AccountsService`; nothing else in `accounts/` changes.
