## Goal
The Overview Upcoming Bill card shows the signed-in user's next 2 upcoming bills (due today or later in their time zone, soonest first), each with the date box, the biller logo, the title, "Last Charge - <date>" and the amount, from a new read-only endpoint in the shared contract that compares due dates with today as a plain-date string. Where Overview scrolls, its last cards end 32 above the end of the page.

8. Given two users, when one user's bills change in the database, then the other's answer is as before; a `userId` in the query changes nothing; no route takes a user or bill ID (#39, #46).
9. Given the backend's query, then today is bound as a `YYYY-MM-DD` string from `todayIn(Clock, zone)`: a bill due today is included where a `Date` parameter would drop it; the backend reads "now" only from `Clock` (#54, #80; task 008 review).

Given it fails after its retries, then "Something went wrong." and Retry show in the card, the rest of the page keeps working, and Retry loads the bills. Given the bills are shown and a later refetch fails, then they stay with no message (#6, #88, #89, #105 a).
11. Given the card, then the frontend formats the cents with `formatMoney` and the dates with `formatDate` and `formatDateBox` and shows the first two bills of the response, with no arithmetic on amounts or dates (#11, #74).
12. Given a screen reader, then the rows are a list of 2 items, each logo an image named "Figma" or "Adobe", each due date a `time` element with its `YYYY-MM-DD` value; nothing in the rows is focusable, and Tab goes from the Goals pencil to "View All" and on, as before (#28, #87).
13. Given Overview at 1440x1024, then the Upcoming Bill card starts at x 1056, y 144, 352 wide, with its rows laid out per #111 a and the three top cards one height. At 1920x1080 and 2560x1440 each row is on one line as drawn, the amount box at the content's right edge, the card and the top row 232 high (#94). At 1280x1024, and at 1440 with a 120-character title and an amount of `$1,000,000,000.00`, nothing overlaps, is cut or leaves the card's content box, and there is no horizontal scroll (#94, #101, #106, #111 a).
14. Given Overview at 1440x1024, then the Upcoming Bill card matches `docs/figma/104. Overview.png`, with the differences listed in Validation step 4.
15.
