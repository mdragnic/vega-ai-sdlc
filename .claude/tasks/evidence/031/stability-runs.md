## Causes and fixes

1. `profile-photo.test.ts`, the 3 MB photo with another user's email (unit, run 2). Reproduced alone on the idle machine: 1 failure in 20 runs of the case. Cause, by experiment: supertest's client (superagent) sends `Connection: close`; the write guard answers 409 before the body is read; Node then closes the socket with the rest of the body unread, the system resets the connection, and the client can lose the answer (on a plain Node server answering 409 at once: 198 of 200 resets with the body sent at once, 5 of 200 with `.attach()`; with `Connection: keep-alive`, 200 of 200 answers). The code review then pointed out that the browser's path has the same `Connection: close`: the Vite dev server's `/api` proxy sends it to the backend (no agent configured). Measured through port 5173 with a 2,000,000-byte photo and another user's `X-Expected-User`: the 409 in 176 of 300 tries, an empty 502 from the proxy 62 times, a reset 62 times. So the app dropped a real user's answer: decision #131 (Marko: option (a)). A first test fix (`Connection: keep-alive` in the file's `patch()`) was reverted at his request, so the unit test also uses the real path. Fix (backend, #131): `ProfileGuardAnswerFilter` on `PATCH /api/profile`: before a guard's 401 or 409 is answered, the unread body is read and dropped up to `PROFILE_DISCARD_LIMIT` (the photo limit plus room for the text parts multer allows, 2,113,512 bytes), never parsed, kept or stored; a larger body is not waited for. Reproducing e2e tests first (`settings-photo.spec.ts`, "a near-2 MB photo save refused by a guard, through the dev server", 100 saves each through 5173): before the fix both failed (502s, resets, then a request hanging to the test timeout); after it, 100 of 100 each, and 10 of 10 over 5 repetitions (1,000 refused saves, every one the real 409 or 401). `security-review` on the change: no findings (`security-review.md`). The unit case without keep-alive after the fix: 60 of 60; the file 30 of 30 (72 tests each).
2. `settings-photo.spec.ts` "never serves backend/data through the dev server" (e2e, repeat 2). Cause: `holdsNoneOf` base64-encoded the whole e2e database file to compare its first 24 characters; after about 800 tests the file was 433,324,032 bytes, and a buffer of 402,653,184 bytes or more gives a base64 string longer than V8 allows (reproduced in Node with the same message). Fix (test only): the first 18 bytes give exactly those 24 characters (3 bytes per 4 characters), checked equal for 700 buffers of 0 to 1,000,000 bytes; the assertion is the same.
3. `goals.spec.ts` "shows each broken rule, one request while saving and a 500" (e2e, repeat 2). Cause: after a submit with a broken rule, react-hook-form 7.90.0 focuses the field with the error, then once more in `setTimeout(_focusError)`, queued before it publishes the errors; the test's `toBeFocused()` is met by the first focus, so the next `presentField.fill('11000')` could be interrupted by the second and type into Target (Target valid, no error, the failure seen). Reproduced in the page, deterministically: Present focused the moment the error shows, focus ends on Target 3 of 3 (with and without the fixture's clock); focused one zero-delay timer later, it stays on Present 3 of 3. Not a user's action: no one types within one zero-delay timer of the error. Fix (test only): the new `afterQueuedTimers(page)` (e2e/helpers.ts) after the error, before typing elsewhere.
4. The known flake, `goals.spec.ts` "opens 110.1 for Mar, 2026 ... and saves the month" (passed in these runs; investigated). Cause: Radix's dismissable layer (1.2.1) adds its `pointerdown` listener on the document in a zero-delay timer queued as the popup mounts (so the click that opened it cannot close it); a press on the dimmed area before that does nothing, and the popup stays open. Measured in the page: the listener comes after the popup's first frame every time, 1 to 2 ms after it normally and 55 to 180 ms after it with the CPU throttled 20 times. Reproduced: a press dispatched as the dialog appears, popup still open 5 of 5; one zero-delay timer later, closed 5 of 5; in Playwright with the CPU throttled 6 times, the outside-press loop failed 10 of 10 as the tests wait now (`toBeFocused()`) and passed 10 of 10 with `afterQueuedTimers`. Not a user's action on a normal machine (1 to 2 ms after the dim area appears); under heavy load the window is what keeps the opening click (a double click on "Adjust Goal") from closing the popup, and the next press closes it. Fix (tests only): the popup helpers wait for the first field's focus (the same effects pass that queues Radix's timer) and `afterQueuedTimers`.
