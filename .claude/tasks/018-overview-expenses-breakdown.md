## Goal
The Overview Expenses Breakdown card shows the six fixed categories in Figma's 3x2 grid, each with its icon, this month's total so far, the whole-number change against all of last month with a red up or green down arrow, and a right arrow that turns teal on hover and opens Expenses. The numbers come from a new read-only endpoint in the shared contract that sums, compares and rounds everything in the backend, with the month in the user's zone, through `VisibleTransactions` only.
