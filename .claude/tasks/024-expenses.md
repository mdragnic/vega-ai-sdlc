## Goal
Expenses (109) shows the signed-in user's Complete expenses per month, Jan to Dec, this year against last year in their time zone, as task 006's bar chart with a backend-computed axis, and below it the six category cards: each with this month's total and change against last month and the category's 2 most recent Complete expenses this month.
